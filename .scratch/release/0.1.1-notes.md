Two fixes for 0.1.0, both found in Live.

## Fixes

- **Live no longer quits when many Locks play at once.** Live keeps the notes a Max device holds in a set of 128, and
  quits when a note goes past it. The device held each Lock as a note until the next ratchet, so a step with many
  Tracks and many Locks could hold several hundred notes (399 for the heaviest Pattern). A Lock played live is now a
  note-on followed at once by its note-off: MD Drums reads a Lock at its note-on, and the device holds at most one note
  per Track, plus the Lock being sent. Two devices playing the heaviest Pattern on one track, which made Live quit two
  seconds after the start with 0.1.0, now play on. Printed clips keep their Locks' lengths.
- **The Track names stay.** The Track tabs, the window's rows and the Lock knobs' Kit values could go back to numbers
  for half a second to twelve seconds, about once a minute. The device now tells a Kit file that MD Drums is replacing
  from a file that is gone, and says hello to MD Drums at its own pace, even when Max's interface thread is busy.

## Requirements and install

As for 0.1.0: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), Live 12 with Max for Live, MD Drums 0.1.0 or later (0.1.1 or later for the
Track names). Unzip `md-drums-seq-0.1.1-win64.zip`, put the `md-drums-seq` folder in Live's User Library under
`Presets/MIDI Effects/Max MIDI Effect/` in place of the old one, and restart Live: Max never loads a new version of an
engine it has already loaded.
