MD Drums Seq becomes MoDule Seq, after the plug-in it plays, with a new interface, Push 2, the Machinedrum's Accent
and Slide, and the capture of what it played.

## New

- **MoDule Seq.** The device, its folder and its repository take the plug-in's new name. Its update check follows the
  new repository; 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 still find this release, through GitHub's redirect.
- **A new interface.** MoDule's colours and Ableton Sans, as Max carries it inside Live: keys that fill with
  MoDule's turquoise once chosen, screens for what the device reports (the Pattern playing and its length, the chain,
  the take, the step shown, what Print did), a muted Track in coral. In the grid and the strip each empty step is a
  key, a Hit is in ink, an accented step is amber; the steps past the Pattern's length are off, a line marks its end,
  and the strip's pages past it cannot be chosen. The device no longer follows Live's theme.
- **Push 2.** With Push on, the device takes Push 2 while it is on and its track is selected: the 32 steps of the
  Track shown on the top four rows of pads, the 16 Tracks and the 16 Patterns below, a held step's Locks on the eight
  encoders. Held, Delete clears a step or a Pattern, Duplicate copies a Pattern over another, Mute (or tapped, to
  latch it) mutes Tracks, Accent marks steps; Double doubles the Pattern. See the README.
- **Accent and Slide, the Machinedrum's way.** One trig row under the grid, as the menu at its left chooses: Swing
  (as before), Accent (every Hit of an accented step plays at 128 + 2 x the Pattern's amount) or Slide (each Track's
  locked values glide to those of its next Hit). They need MoDule 0.1.2 or later.
- **Capture.** Capture writes the last take, what the device played since the transport last started, into a
  Session clip per Track named "Take n", placed as Print places its clips. Print can now write it too: its menu chooses
  the Pattern shown over 1 to 16 Passes, or the last take, and its button prints.
- **Clips only.** A switch, and a Live parameter, with which the device sends no note-on: printed or captured clips
  then play MoDule alone.

## Changed

- The Tracks of a user's bank are named: MoDule's User Machines, and a ROM Machine playing a user's Sample, after
  that Sample's file. A long name is cut to its place, with an ellipsis, rather than broken on two lines.

- A chain is played by the sequencer itself: each Pass that begins queues the chain's next Pattern, so that a
  Pattern of one step plays its step and a busy Live never skips or repeats one. Stopped and started again, the chain
  goes on from the Pattern after the one played.

- The edit window's grid is drawn only while the window shows, and the update check runs once a day for every
  device of a set together.

- The hello with which the device tells MoDule where to write its Kit is rarer in the MIDI it sends while stopped:
  every ten seconds once MoDule answers (five before), and from one second to ten without an answer (every second
  before).

## Requirements and install

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), Live 12 with Max for Live, MoDule (or MD Drums) 0.1.0 or later, 0.1.1 or later for the
Track names, 0.1.2 or later for the Accent and the Slide. Unzip `MModuleSeq-0.2.0-win64.zip`, put the `MoDule Seq`
folder in Live's User Library under `Presets/MIDI Effects/Max MIDI Effect/`, remove the older `md-drums-seq` folder
(its engine has the same name, and Max loads only one), and restart Live: Max never loads a new version of an engine
it has already loaded. In a set made with MD Drums Seq, put MoDule Seq in its place.
